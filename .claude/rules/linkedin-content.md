# LinkedIn Content

## Purpose

Content created for LinkedIn is aimed at clients and prospects.

## Guidelines

- Keep it professional, accurate, clear, and concise.
- Use simple language and short sentences.
- No emojis, no em-dashes.
- Write in a neutral, humanlike voice. Avoid AI-sounding phrasing.
- Use short paragraphs and bullet lists where useful.
- Position Livnot Lab's digital solutions (design, websites, AI agents, automation) clearly.
- Every post should support the goal of getting clients.
